Our Process

Conversation precedes creation.

Useful things begin by understanding the real problem before deciding what should be built.

01

Conversation

We begin with questions, observations, and the actual needs of the user.

02

Design

The idea becomes a sketch, model, plan, workflow, or prototype.

03

Build

We choose the right materials and transform the concept into reality.

04

Test

Real-world use reveals what works, what does not, and what should improve.

05

Launch

The finished idea becomes something useful, repeatable, and scalable.
