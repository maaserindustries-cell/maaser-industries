FAQ

Before you reserve.

Does the same business appear on both sides?

No. The template and design language are consistent on both sides, but the featured advertisements are different. Micro mentions are repeated on both sides.

How many featured businesses are on the card?

The standard template provides 16 equal featured spaces per side, for up to 32 featured businesses across a double-sided card.

Do I need finished artwork?

No. Provide your logo, one clear offer, your contact information, and any preferred imagery. Artwork assistance is included.

Can my advertisement include a QR code?

A featured advertisement may include a QR code when the layout allows. Micro mentions do not include individual QR codes because the space is intentionally compact.

What happens if my category is already taken?

You may choose another edition or reserve a future campaign. Category availability is confirmed before the advertisement is finalized.
