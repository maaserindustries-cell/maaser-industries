FAQ

Before you reserve.

What does category-exclusive mean?

Only one participating business from a defined category appears on that specific card.

Do I need finished artwork?

No. You can provide your logo, offer, contact details, and preferred imagery. Design assistance can be included.

What happens if my category is already taken?

You can choose another campaign, reserve a future mailing, or request a transfer or refund if a conflict was not caught before checkout.

Can this expand to other cities?

Yes. The location and campaign structure is designed so additional markets can be added without rebuilding the entire page.

Will AI help build the ad?

The planned self-service platform will provide expert and AI-assisted recommendations for offers, copy, images, and layout.
