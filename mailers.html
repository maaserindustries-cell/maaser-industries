Questions

What businesses usually ask.

What does category-exclusive mean?

Only one participating business from a defined category appears on that specific card.

Do I need to design my own ad?

No. You can provide your logo, offer, contact details, and preferred imagery. The layout can be coordinated for you.

Can I participate in more than one campaign?

Yes, provided your category is still available on each selected card.

Will this expand beyond St. Augustine?

Yes. The same system is designed to be repeated in additional cities after the local campaign is validated.
